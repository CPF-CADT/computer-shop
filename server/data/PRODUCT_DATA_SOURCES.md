# Product seed data sources

The 20 catalog records in `scripts/seedCatalog.ts` are deployment-demo data. Prices are reasonable USD catalog estimates, not live retailer quotes.

Product names and specifications were selected from the TechFuelHQ PC Builder Parts Dataset, licensed CC BY 4.0:

- https://github.com/iBlessi/pc-builder-parts
- Attribution: TechFuelHQ PC Builder Parts Dataset (CC BY 4.0), https://techfuelhq.com/tools/pc-builder/

Category reference images are hosted on Cloudinary after seeding. They are representative of each component category and do not claim to show the exact retail model.
